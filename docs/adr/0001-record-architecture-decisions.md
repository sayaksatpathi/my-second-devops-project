# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
OpsForge makes many non-obvious technology and design choices. Future readers
(and reviewers) need to understand *why*, not just *what*.

## Decision
We record each significant decision as a short Architecture Decision Record
(ADR) in `docs/adr/`, numbered sequentially.

## Consequences
Decisions are traceable; trade-offs are explicit; onboarding is faster.
